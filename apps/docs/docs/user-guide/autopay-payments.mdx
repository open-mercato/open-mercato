This guide walks you through connecting your own Autopay account to Open Mercato and running your first sandbox session. Unlike some other providers, Open Mercato does not ship a shared Autopay account — every merchant registers independently.

## Before you start

This is a **self-service integration**: `@open-mercato/gateway-autopay` is a reusable provider package, not a bespoke integration tied to one merchant's account. To use it you register your own Autopay Partner Service and enter your own credentials — Open Mercato never ships or shares credentials on your behalf.

1. Register a Partner Service at [portal.autopay.eu](https://portal.autopay.eu/) (start in the sandbox/test environment).
2. From that registration you receive:
   - a **Service ID**,
   - a **shared key** (used to sign and verify every request — keep it secret),
   - a **payment initiation URL** specific to your registration (Autopay does not publish one universal URL; yours is issued to you).
3. Confirm with Autopay whether your Partner Service has **settlement balance / refunds enabled** if you plan to use the refund feature (see [Limitations](#limitations-read-before-you-rely-on-this) below).

:::caution
The Autopay documentation's own worked examples use `ServiceID=2` and shared key `2test2` to demonstrate the hash calculation. These values only prove the signing math is correct — they will not authenticate against Autopay's sandbox or process a real session. You need your own registration.
:::

## Step 1 — Enter Autopay credentials

1. Navigate to **External Systems → Integrations** and click **Configure** on the Autopay card.
2. Open the **Credentials** tab.
3. Fill in the fields from your own Autopay Partner Service registration:

| Field | Where it comes from |
|-------|---------------------|
| **Service ID** | Issued when you register at [portal.autopay.eu](https://portal.autopay.eu/) |
| **Shared Key** | Issued alongside the Service ID — keep this secret |
| **Hash Algorithm** | SHA-256 by default; only change this if Autopay configured SHA-512 for your Service |
| **Payment Initiation URL** | The exact hosted-session URL Autopay gives you — never guess or reuse another merchant's URL. Must be under `https://testpay.autopay.eu` (sandbox) or `https://pay.autopay.eu` (production) |

4. Click **Save Credentials**.

## Step 2 — Verify health

Switch to the **Health** tab and click **Run Check**. Open Mercato probes Autopay's `transactionStatus` endpoint with a synthetic order ID that can never match a real transaction — this proves your Service ID, shared key, and gateway URL are valid without creating or affecting any real payment.

If the check fails, double-check that the Payment Initiation URL's host matches exactly `https://testpay.autopay.eu` or `https://pay.autopay.eu`, and that the shared key matches what Autopay issued for that Service ID.

## Optional env preconfiguration

If your deployment already knows the Autopay credentials, you can preconfigure the integration automatically instead of filling the credentials form manually after every fresh install.

Use these env vars:

```env
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_SERVICE_ID=your-service-id
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_SHARED_KEY=your-shared-key
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_GATEWAY_URL=https://testpay.autopay.eu/your-issued-path
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_HASH_ALGORITHM=sha256
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_ENABLED=true
OM_INTEGRATION_AUTOPAY_FORCE_PRECONFIGURE=false
```

To rerun the preset for an existing tenant:

```bash
yarn mercato gateway_autopay configure-from-env --tenant <tenantId> --org <organizationId>
```

## What this integration does

- Creates a hosted-redirect PLN payment session — the payer completes payment on Autopay's own page (bank transfer, BLIK, cards), then returns to your store.
- Reads payment status by actively querying Autopay's `transactionStatus` API (used on the payer's return, or any time you want to check).
- Cancels a session that has not been paid yet.
- Requests a refund on an already-settled session (see the note below — this is asynchronous).

## Limitations (read before you rely on this)

- **PLN only.** Other currencies are not supported by this package.
- **No push notifications (ITN) yet.** Autopay can notify Open Mercato the moment a payment completes, but this requires the merchant's endpoint to reply with a specific, provider-signed XML body that Open Mercato's generic webhook route does not yet produce. Until that follow-up ships, status is read by actively calling `transactionStatus` (on payer return, or via your own polling) rather than by push.
- **No separate capture step.** Autopay settles immediately when the payer completes the redirect — there is nothing to capture afterward.
- **Refunds are asynchronous.** A refund request is acknowledged immediately, but Autopay processes it in the background (documented as up to ~30 minutes). Open Mercato reports the refund as pending until a follow-up status check confirms it. Refunds also require your Autopay Partner Service to have settlement balance enabled — confirm this with Autopay if a refund is rejected unexpectedly.
- **Sandbox testing needs your own Autopay sandbox registration.** There is no shared/demo account — see [Before you start](#before-you-start).

## Programmatic usage

This provider implements the same `GatewayAdapter` contract as every other payment gateway. For building custom checkout flows or server-to-server integrations, see the [Payment Gateways REST API](/api/payment-gateways) reference — everything there (`POST /payment_gateways/sessions` with `providerKey: "autopay"`, status polling, cancel/refund) applies the same way it does for Stripe.
