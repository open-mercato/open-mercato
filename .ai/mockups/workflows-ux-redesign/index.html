What changes here
  1. 1Auto-layout left→right with persisted arrangement — the flow reopens exactly as arranged; “Tidy” re-runs layout on demand.#4248 · §4.1
  2. 2Agent nodes expose labeled outcome handles (approved / needs review / + outcome) — branching on disposition is wiring, not context string-matching. Unwired outcomes follow the node’s error directive.§7.2
  3. 3Routes carry their logic visibly: condition chips and activity chips sit on the edge. Drag an action onto a route to attach it; drag an endpoint to reattach the route.#4244 · #4233 · §4.4
  4. 4Errors are routes too — the red dashed edge is a first-class error route with a directive (escalate), never a silent fail-stop.§5.9 · §8
  5. 5Eager validation: the node wears its error count; nothing saves silently broken. Draft autosave is separate from Publish.#4232 · §4.3/§4.7
  6. 6Config at a glance: every node shows its registry icon and a one-line summary (command id, role, signal, deadline) — no more opening modals to know what a step does.§4.2
  7. 7The Problems panel lists every issue with a jump link — replacing the single-error flash toast.#4232 · §4.3
