# Accessibility audit materials

Product contract: [WCAG 2.2 AA specification](../../specs/2026-10-01-wcag-22-aa-accessibility.md). Related plans: [WCAG Guardian](../../specs/2026-10-01-wcag-guardian-pr-review.md) and [Storybook quality gates](../../specs/2026-10-01-storybook-wcag-quality-gates.md).

- [Criterion index](wcag-22-aa-matrix.csv): all 55 WCAG 2.2 A/AA criteria with the surface family they usually concern and the planned evaluation methods. It carries no status or evidence columns; it is not the product evaluation ledger.
- [EN 301 549 and directive supplement](en-301-549-supplement.csv): the requirements outside the 55 criteria that apply to a web product and its deployers, with the clause numbers of EN 301 549 V3.2.1 and V4.1.1 and the condition under which each applies. Identifiers are `ENS-*` (EN 301 549), `EAA-*` (Directive (EU) 2019/882), and `WAD-*` (Directive (EU) 2016/2102). An empty clause column means the requirement comes from a directive and has no clause; “not in Table A.1” means the clause exists in V3.2.1 but its Table A.1 does not apply it to web content; “informative” and “void” mean the clause is not a requirement in that version. `implementedBy` says who has to act: `product`, `deployer with product support` (the deployer acts and the product must make that possible), or `product and deployer`. Like the criterion index, the file carries no status or evidence.
- [Scope manifest template](scope-manifest.template.json): release/build, standards baseline, delivered modules, supported configurations, complete processes, the accessibility-support baseline, time limits, generated documents, outbound messages, authoring surfaces, templates, documentation and support channels, third-party embeds, the applicability decision for every supplement row, owners and required checks. Populate and approve before the baseline audit.
- [Evaluation ledger template](evaluation-records.template.csv): one record per criterion, surface, flow, configuration, state, method and snapshot. Missing or stale required evidence prevents a pass. The `criterionId` column holds a WCAG success criterion number or a supplement identifier; a supplement row that does not apply is recorded as `not_applicable` with the evidence that its condition is false. A document or message that fails a clause 10 requirement is recorded under the WCAG criterion it mirrors, with the document as the surface; `ENS-14` records applicability and the document-specific clauses. Each of the five conformance requirements has its own `ENS-12` record, named in `requiredCheckId`.

Method values in `plannedMethods` (joined with `+`) and in the ledger `method` column are `source`, `automated`, `browser`, `measurement`, `manual` and `manual_at`, as defined in the [report contract](../../skills/om-wcag-guardian/references/review.md#methods). `automated` covers only the part of a criterion a tool can detect and never closes a criterion alone. Ledger `status` values are `not_evaluated`, `pass`, `fail` and `not_applicable`; a record that does not exist is `not_evaluated`.

These are versioned instructions and empty templates, not collected audit evidence or a conformance claim. Keep executable integration tests in module `__integration__` directories. Keep heavy or private operational evidence in an approved artifact store, sanitized and referenced by hash; do not commit customer data, credentials, local paths or session reports.

The skill source is at `.ai/skills/om-wcag-guardian/`, registered in the opt-in `analysis` tier. Default installation, review routing and CI integration remain unimplemented.
