## Customer groups Phases 3–4 (2026-10-26)

Source: `ANALYSIS-2026-10-26-customer-groups-release-5.md`, findings G4, G5, G7, G8, G10, G13, G15, G16. Walked through one decision at a time.

| Id | Finding | Decision | Consequence for the specs |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-R5-1 | G4, G5, G24 | **Both sides can approve, configured per group.** New `CustomerGroupTerms.approval_policy`: `buyer \| merchant \| both`. Nullable, inherited from the parent chain like `allow_purchase_on_account`; the tenant default is `buyer`. No `either` value. With `both`, the two sides sign **in any order**: the request is approved when both slots are `approved`, and one rejection from either side ends it as `rejected`. The request snapshots the policy at creation, so changing the group's terms does not change the rules for a pending request. Buyer-side deciders are `CustomerUser`s of the approval's company holding the portal decide feature; merchant-side deciders are staff holding `customer_groups.approvals.decide`. `CustomerEntityRole` is not the approver model; its `account_manager` rows only route staff notifications. Requester and decider columns carry an actor type (`customer_user \| staff_user`), since a staff rep can author a cart through the proposal seam. A requester cannot decide their own request. | customer-groups §5.3 (new column), §5.6 (two decision slots, actor-typed columns, policy snapshot), §6 (approval service), §9 (portal routes); checkout §5.1.2 / §8.2 (approver can be either side) |
| D-R5-2 | G7 | **Hybrid settlement.** `customer_groups` subscribes to `sales.order.cancelled` and releases the order's unsettled reservation automatically. Payments are **not** settled automatically: they are settled manually through `/adjust` or by an integration calling `settleCredit`. The documentation states plainly that without an integration, exposure after payment is settled manually. | customer-groups new §6.5 (credit lifecycle), §11 (subscriber), user docs; checkout §8.3 ("until invoicing settles it" becomes "until a payment is settled through `/adjust` or `settleCredit`") |
| D-R5-3 | G8 | **Only a higher total invalidates an approval.** Approval is consent to spend up to an amount in a currency. Changing lines at the same or a lower total keeps it; a total above the approved amount re-requests approval (checkout §8.2, R8). No `subject_digest`. The cart is the canonical subject; checkout reuses `Cart.approvalId` and does not create its own approval. `checkout_session` leaves the `subject_type` enum. | customer-groups §5.6 (`subject_type: cart \| quote`); cart §9/§11; checkout §4.1, §8.2 |
| D-R5-4 | G10 | **`terms_currency_code`.** New nullable, inherited column on `CustomerGroupTerms`, the currency of `approval_required_above` and `min_order_value`. A cart in another currency always requires approval (fail-closed), and `min_order_value` is not checked for it. No FX conversion, same precedent as D-R4-27. | customer-groups §5.3, §6 `ResolvedTerms` (+ `termsCurrencyCode` and its `sources` entry); cart F4 `requiresApproval` computation; checkout `min_order_value` check |
| D-R5-5 | G13 | **Credit accounts are created by an operator only.** With no account, `checkCredit` returns `no_account` and checkout offers prepayment or a quote (US-B3). `default_credit_limit` only pre-fills the account form. A banner on the credit panel lists customers whose resolved terms allow purchase on account but who have no account in the terms currency, modelled on the Phase 1 orphan banner. | customer-groups §5.3 (`default_credit_limit` semantics), §5.4, §7.3 / §17 Epic E (banner) |
| D-R5-6 | G15 | **E-mail with a per-tenant switch.** Buyer-side deciders and requesters (`CustomerUser`) are e-mailed through the shared `sendEmail` pipeline, on by default and switchable off per tenant through `ModuleConfigService` (for integrators who notify through their own storefront). `approval.*` events always carry `portalBroadcast: true` and are available to webhooks. Staff get in-app notifications: approval requests go to merchant deciders, and `credit.limit_exceeded` goes to the company's `account_manager` (`CustomerEntityRole`), falling back to staff holding `credit.manage`. Every eligible decider of a company is notified; the first decision closes the slot (CAS, G6). | customer-groups §10 (notification type ids, payloads), new `notifications.ts`, e-mail templates and i18n |
| D-R5-7 | G16 | **Least privilege.** `employee` gets `customer_groups.credit.view` and `customer_groups.approvals.view` only; `credit.manage`, `credit.adjust` and `approvals.decide` stay with `admin` (`customer_groups.*`) or are granted by hand. Portal grants via `defaultCustomerRoleFeatures`: `portal_admin` views and decides its company's approvals; `buyer` views and withdraws its own requests; `viewer` gets nothing. Existing tenants need `yarn mercato auth sync-role-acls` and `yarn mercato customer_accounts sync-customer-role-acls`. | customer-groups §9.1 (features + portal features), `setup.ts` grant plan, §8.4 (sync steps) |

## Availability Phase 3 (2026-10-26)

Source: `ANALYSIS-2026-10-26-availability-release-5.md`, findings A5, A6, A14. Walked through one decision at a time.

| Id | Finding | Decision | Consequence for the specs |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-R5-8 | A5, A8 | **Commit at document creation.** Checkout runs `availability.commit` right after step 4 creates the order, before payment. The checkout hold becomes an `order` reservation, which nothing expires. A failed or abandoned payment is compensated by cancelling the order, and `wms` already releases `order` holds on `sales.order.cancelled`. Because commit always precedes confirmation, the §4.4 confirmed-first path leaves the checkout flow; it is kept only for integrators who confirm first. `commit` and the expiry sweep transition the hold by CAS on `status = 'active'`. A commit that still loses tries one fresh reserve and, on shortfall, raises `stock_lost_after_payment` for ops. | availability §4.1, §4.4, §7 (timeline: hold from submit to document, then order hold until payment or cancel), §10; checkout §7.2 (commit moves from steps 7/8 to after step 4), §7.3 (payment-failure compensation = cancel order, which releases stock), R3 |
| D-R5-9 | A6 | **Backorder holds what is sellable.** A backorder-enabled line holds `min(requested, sellable)` and admits the rest as backorder with nothing held. The per-line result carries `heldQuantity` and `backorderQuantity`. Lines that are not stock-managed (or any line when `wms` is absent) return `not_tracked`, and preorder lines before `releaseAt` return `preorder`. Both hold nothing and do not fail the reserve. `reserved` is true when no line has a shortfall. | availability §4.1 (per-line `outcome`), §12; checkout §7.2 step 2 (abort only on `shortfall` lines), checkout Open Question 2 narrowed to non-backorder partial fulfilment |
| D-R5-10 | A14 | **Not undoable.** `availability.reserve`, `.release` and `.commit` are registered with `isUndoable: false`, like `wms.inventory.reserve/release`. They are audited, and reversal is an explicit counter-action: release with a reason, or the checkout compensation table (§7.3). No audit-log Undo can return a committed order hold to `checkout` or release stock under a live session. | availability Reconciliation note, §4.1, §16 (command-pattern row), §17 US-D2 |
