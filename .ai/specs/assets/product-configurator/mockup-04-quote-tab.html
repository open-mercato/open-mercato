Quote: configurations tab

Entry point for the rep and the place to continue, change, copy and track configurations.
1
StartOpens a new configuration. Needs permission to manage configurations and view the quote.
2
In progressUnfinished configurations and copies with who started them and when. Anyone allowed on the quote can continue or discard them.
3
Copies and editsAdd another identical opens the workspace as Copy of, with the same choices and today’s price; Add to quote adds a separate line with new-line defaults. To change a machine in v1, add a changed copy, re-enter what differs in Edit line details and remove the old line in Items.
4
Phase E: re-configureRe-configure in place ships after v1; one re-configuration per line at a time.
5
Phase E: replacement pendingOne card for the new line; it names the old line with its price, and the old line has no actions of its own. Retry finishes it and never adds a third line.
6
Line removedThe quote line was deleted in Items; the details stay as history. Configure again starts a copy that is added as a new line, because the old line’s terms are gone.
7
Phase E: historyConfigurations replaced by a newer one, struck through.
8
Model pickerOnly when several models fit. Esc cancels, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter starts.
9
Phase E: warning outside the tabWhile two lines exist for one configuration, a warning shows in the quote details spot, which sales renders below the totals. A higher placement is asked for in Q5.
10
Nothing fitsKnown when the tab loads, not after a click. An API error shows as an error, not as this state.
11
Copy reviewOnly when the model changed since the copied configuration: the choices that moved, a review checkbox and Add to quote. No commercial terms, because a copy does not carry them.
