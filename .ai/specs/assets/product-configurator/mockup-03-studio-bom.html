Studio: bill of materials

Admin defines which catalog parts make up the machine and in what quantity. Shown with one draft change that fails a check.
1
SKU from a lookupOne BOM line resolves to different catalog SKUs depending on a choice. A missing row for a selected option blocks the configuration; it never drops the part silently.
2
Formula quantityQuantity can come from a formula, e.g. metres of boom extension.
3
ConditionsA part is left out only by an explicit condition.
4
Units from the catalogTitles and units are read from the catalog while a row is edited. A fixed SKU shows its catalog unit, read-only. A lookup line needs one common unit across its SKUs. No unit conversion in v1.
5
Surcharge bindingShows the rule’s friendly name with its id.
6
Row editorEvery field is a form control; the unit is shown, not typed. JSON stays only as an advanced view.
