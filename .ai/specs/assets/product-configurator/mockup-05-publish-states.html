Studio: publish and conflict states

What the admin sees when the draft from the formulas and BOM screens is validated, and when two people edit at once.
1
Concurrent editSaving is refused (409) and the standard conflict bar appears. Refresh asks first and offers a copy of the local draft, so work is never lost by one click.
2
Validation reportValidate and Publish run the same checks on the saved draft; all problems are listed together.
3
Jump to the causeEach problem links to the tab and row to fix. Rules show their friendly name with the id.
4
VersionsPublishing never changes quotes already made or configurations in progress.
