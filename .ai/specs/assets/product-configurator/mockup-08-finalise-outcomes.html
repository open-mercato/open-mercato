Configurator: adding to the quote

What the rep sees after clicking Add to quote, for success and for the cases where it cannot complete.
1
SuccessBack on the Configurations tab with the new line highlighted and focused.
2
Price changedAdd to quote sends the result the rep saw. If catalog prices changed, nothing is written and the rep confirms the new price.
3
Concurrent changeEvery change carries a revision; an older one is refused. The rep’s rejected choices are kept and shown next to the current ones, to keep or apply again.
4
Quote editedNothing is written; the choices stay.
5
Quote convertedThe configuration cannot finish; the message offers a way out instead of a retry that cannot work.
6
Another add runningOne add per quote at a time; no duplicate line.
7
Already addedAdded in another window: the stored result is used and the quote opens with the line highlighted. Nobody is named.
8
PermissionsConfiguring needs quote view rights; adding needs quote edit rights.
9
Cannot finishThe message names the actual reason (model deleted, product missing or currency changed); Discard is the way out.
10
Write failedA transient write failure keeps the choices and offers Try again; no duplicate line is ever created.
11
Phase E: partly doneThe new line is written but the old one is still there: not announced as full success.
12
Discarded elsewhereSomeone discarded this configuration in another window; nothing was added.
13
Phase E: source line deletedThe line being replaced was deleted in Items; Configure again adds the configuration as a new line without the old terms.
