Re-configure: review before replacing

Phase E (after v1). Rep re-configures a line made with model v3 after v4 was published. Replace line in the workspace opens this review; nothing is written before Replace line here.
1
What is replacedThe header names the line being replaced and the version jump. A configuration in progress stays on the version it was opened with.
2
Choices that movedValues the new version no longer allows are listed as dropped or reset, never swapped silently.
3
Commercial terms keptName, comment, discount and tax come from the old line. A fixed discount amount needs a keep or remove decision because the price changed.
4
Confirm, then replaceShown before every replace. Edit choices goes back to the workspace. The rep confirms the changed choices; the new line replaces the old one and the old one moves to history.
5
Fixed discountA percentage carries over as is. A fixed amount was negotiated on the old price, so the rep keeps or removes it explicitly.
