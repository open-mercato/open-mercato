Configurator workspace

Sales rep configures a machine from a quote. Shown mid-way, with one blocking message, plus the other result states below.
1
ContextQuote number and customer come from the quote; the session belongs to one quote and one published model version. Refresh restores the same state.
2
Controls by typeUp to 5 options: radio group; more: select; yes/no: switch; colours: swatch radios; numbers: text field with unit. Arrow keys move inside a group.
3
NumbersThe field keeps what you type and sends a valid number on Enter, blur or a short pause.
4
Disabled option with reasonGreyed but focusable; the reason is read by screen readers. Sending it through the API is rejected too.
5
Value set by a ruleThe other value is locked with the reason in plain words. If the rule stops applying, the user’s own choice comes back.
6
Conditional fieldAppears only when Paint = Custom colour. Hidden fields keep the user’s value and stay out of the summary.
7
MessagesBlocking messages stop adding to the quote, warnings do not. “Go to” scrolls to and highlights the field. Changes are announced once per evaluation.
8
BOMCollapsible; parts and quantities from the model, prices from the catalog for this quote’s channel and customer.
9
Sticky summaryPrice, status and the main action stay in view on long models; on a narrow screen they become a bottom bar.
10
Leave or discardBack to quote keeps the work. Discard configuration ends it after a confirmation.
11
Add to quoteDisabled while a blocking message is shown; sends the result the rep saw, so a price change is never applied silently.
12
Field errorRange and step errors appear under the field, not as a general error. Add to quote stays disabled until every field holds a valid, evaluated value.
13
EvaluatingThe previous result is greyed and the main action disabled, so an old price never looks current.
14
Missing priceNames the part and channel and says who can fix it. No Retry: it would fail the same way.
15
Model problemFormula, rule or forced-value errors point to an admin fix.
16
Transient errorOnly network and server errors offer Retry.
17
Copy across versionsAdd another identical opens the workspace as Copy of. Choices the new version changed are marked from the start; Add to quote first shows the copy review (quote tab wireframe). Phase E: the same workspace with a Replacing header and Replace line.
