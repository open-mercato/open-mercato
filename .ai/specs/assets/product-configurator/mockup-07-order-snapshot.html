Order: what was sold

Back office sees configured lines in the order items and opens the details. Phase E adds the warning about a line replaced before conversion.
1
Configured columnAdded to the order items through the existing order-items column host; links to the configuration details.
2
Phase E: two lines warnedIf the quote was converted in the middle of a replacement, the old line is marked Replaced in the Configured column, and a warning shows in the order details spot (rendered below the totals by sales).
3
DetailsOpened from the order with order access plus permission to view configurations. The heading is the order line name (the model title if the line is gone). Model version is shown as reference; later model edits do not change it.
4
What to buildAll parts and quantities with the prices used when the line was added.
5
Order tabRead-only list of configured lines. Phase E: a line replaced before conversion is marked with what to do.
