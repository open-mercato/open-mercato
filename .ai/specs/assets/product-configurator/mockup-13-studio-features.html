Studio: features

Admin defines what the rep can choose: type, options, limits, grouping, defaults and when a field is shown.
1
Name and keyPeople see the name; formulas, rules and lookups use the key.
2
TypeChoice, yes/no, number or colour. The type decides the control the rep gets (see the configurator wireframe).
3
In summaryMarks the fields that build the line summary on the quote.
4
Options from a lookupA choice can take its options and default from a permitted lookup keyed by another feature.
5
Shown whenConditions use the same builder as rules; a hidden field keeps the rep’s value.
6
Row editorEvery property is a form control; a colour option carries a validated swatch.
7
NumbersRange, step, unit and default; the rep gets errors under the field when out of range or step.
