Quote: editing a configured line

Commercial details of a configured line are edited from the Configurations tab. The standard Items dialog cannot save a configured line.
1
Edit from the tabA small form in the Configurations tab saves through the sales line command with the quote lock, so sales recalculates totals as for any line.
2
What can changeName, comment, discount (none, percent or amount in the quote currency) and tax rate from the sales tax rates.
3
What cannotQuantity, unit and price are shown read-only with the way to change them. The server rejects such changes from any other path too.
4
Standard Items dialogNot changed in v1: it requires a variant and has no discount or comment field, so it cannot save a configured line.
5
Notice below the totalsA widget in the quote details spot tells the rep where configured lines are edited, with a link. The dialog itself cannot show it in v1: its field widgets do not know which line is open (Q5).
6
Order linesConfigured order lines are not edited; the order items show them in the Configured column (order wireframe).
