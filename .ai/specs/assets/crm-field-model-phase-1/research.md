# CRM field model Phase 1: external research

Research date and access date for every source below: **2026-10-22**. This note informs issue #7700's design; the capability specs define the implementation contract.

## Method and evidence limits

The comparison covers Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Odoo, SuiteCRM and EspoCRM. This is a purposive comparison of established commercial and open-source products, not a market-share ranking or an exhaustive feature audit. Product claims below come from primary vendor documentation. Six dated community posts supply first-person experience reports. Those reports establish that a workflow problem was reported, not its prevalence, verified cause, severity across customers, or continued existence in every current edition. Features, editions and configuration differ.

Seventeen external sources are retained. All were opened and inspected; Odoo's rendered documentation timed out, so the same vendor's versioned 19.0 documentation source was inspected directly. Dates are the displayed publication/update dates where available, not estimates from search-engine crawl labels. No source dated after the research date is used. The ICO page explicitly says its guidance is under review; it is used narrowly for evidence-record design, not to certify legal compliance or choose a lawful basis for a tenant.

## Decisions the evidence supports

1. **Separate identity, convenience and permission.** A person owns contact points; the primary field is a compatible convenience projection. Purpose/channel/contact-point evidence and suppression determine contactability. Legacy booleans are retained as provenance rather than upgraded to supported consent evidence (P2, P3, P7, P11; U2, U6).
2. **Protect data before enrichment writes.** An identifier lookup returns candidates and provenance. A reviewable field diff plus atomic, version-checked apply protects existing values. Normalization, duplicate warnings and provider support are separate concerns; successful matching does not necessarily certify a company's legal or tax status (P5; U1).
3. **Use explicit business timestamps and transitions.** Forecast dates, actual closure, interaction occurrence and record modification answer different questions. Closure state must be synchronized through the shared deal command and retain history across reopening; recency must be recomputed when eligible interactions are edited, deleted or relinked (P8-P10; U3).
4. **Make money's source of truth visible.** Catalog links supply initial snapshots; line values belong to the deal. A visible manual/line-items mode decides which amount wins. All mutation paths use the same arithmetic and currency rules, with no silent changes from catalog edits (P4, P6; U5).
5. **Reduce ambiguous classification without destroying customizations.** A lifecycle answers progress, relationship types answer commercial roles, health answers relationship condition, and hierarchy answers organizational ownership. Preserve legacy/tenant values and expose unresolved migration cases; do not deduce outcomes from display labels after migration (P1, P8; U4).

## Applicability and remaining limits

- These sources justify user needs and failure cases, not Mercato's exact schema, module placement, ACL, encryption, event names or migration mechanism. Repository architecture and backward-compatibility rules control those choices.
- No inference is made that all seven products implement every proposed capability, or that a feature's absence from these selected pages proves it is unavailable. There is no prevalence estimate or claim that one vendor is universally better.
- Immutable catalog snapshots, atomic primary mirroring, cycle protection, explicit amount modes, conservative migration and append-only histories are recommended engineering consequences. Where the sources document only related behavior, the table labels that distinction.
- Consent storage and effective contactability are technical capabilities. Commercial outreach must use the guard at dispatch time; a UI warning alone cannot enforce suppression. This research does not establish a universal legal basis, a universal retention period, or a compliance guarantee.
- Later country-specific provider packages need their own source/authority validation. This comparison does not authorize Poland-specific providers or hard-code national identifiers into core.
