Twenty backend screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-08-21-buyer-scoped-catalog-visibility.md and its User Stories section (US-A1–US-F1). The shell, DataTable chrome and form layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. Every screen lists the spec sections and stories it covers.

Screens 9–13 and 15 cover the per-customer override added to the spec on 2026-09-16 (§3.6, §4.4, US-A4): the “Assortment” section customer_groups injects into the customer detail page, in both of its directions, and the diagnose trace that names a customer’s own rule beside their groups’. Screens 18–20 illustrate buyer-facing consequences this spec specifies but whose UI sibling specs own — drawn schematically, without backend chrome, and labelled as illustrative.
