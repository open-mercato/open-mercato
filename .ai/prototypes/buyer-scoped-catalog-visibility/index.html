9. Visibility Diagnose — verdict (union across groups, AND within one scope)

Buyer Dana Ruiz (Ironclad Supply Co.) checked against "M8 Hex Bolt (Bulk Pack, 500ct)." Demonstrates both hard-won rules in one trace: Tier 1's AND-of-dimensions fails despite a partial match, while Tier 2 still grants it — proving the union is a real OR across sources, not a flattened AND (§3.1/§3.2).

Reading this trace
Tier 1 alone would say "no" — its own AND rule isn't satisfied. But the buyer's effective scope is the OR of all three groups' own verdicts (§3.1), and Tier 2 alone is enough to grant it. Flattening all three groups into one merged scope (the mistake an earlier draft made, §3.1) would have wrongly required both Bulk Goods and a tag on every branch — this trace is the concrete case that catches that regression.
