Assisted Selling Rep Console — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-09-22-assisted-selling.md (spec 13 of the ecommerce suite, PR #6346). Scope is the rep console only — the backoffice surface under packages/core/src/modules/assisted_selling/backend/ (§7). The buyer-facing storefront proposal tray and conversation panel are specified in storefront-app.md §5.8–§5.9 and are a separate, out-of-scope prototype surface. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, tabs and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; the AI approval card mirrors packages/ui/src/ai/parts/MutationPreviewCard.tsx. Every screen lists its spec-section and behavioral coverage.
