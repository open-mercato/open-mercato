Bookings — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-08-10-bookings-module.md (version 4.3). All four phases are shown, each screen tagged with its phase; the phase filter above narrows the set. Phase 1 is the first delivery; phases 2–4 are the roadmap the specification describes. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. Sample data is fictional.
