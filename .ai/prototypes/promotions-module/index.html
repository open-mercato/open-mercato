Promotions Module — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/specs/SPEC-055-2026-02-23-promotions-module.md. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. The rule-tree builder, promotion cards and tier rows have no existing DS primitive — they are proposed compositions built only from DS tokens. Every screen lists its requirement and user-story coverage. Cashier/cart (US-G) and storefront code-entry (US-H) are non-UI/non-backend stories and are not represented here.
