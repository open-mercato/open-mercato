Deals Close Timeline — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/prototypes/deals-close-timeline/REQUIREMENTS.md. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. Every screen lists its requirement and user-story coverage.

1. Timeline: weekly columns, lanes by owner (default)

A sales manager opens the Timeline tab to see which open deals close in the coming weeks and whose they are. Each column is one week, and each deal is a compact Kanban card with its exact close date. The cards on this screen are live: drag one into another week, press Esc mid-drag to cancel, or focus one and press Enter for the exact-date dialog.

1Columns are time buckets, not a day axis. The window shows last week (so recent slips stay visible), this week and three weeks ahead, and the current week is tinted. The card's date line carries the exact day, so its width never suggests a duration.
2The card is a compact DealCard from the Kanban (title, probability, value, close date, company) at about 4.5rem instead of about 11rem. Quick actions, activity badge and days-in-stage are left out to keep the height down.

5Dragging into another week shifts the date by whole weeks and keeps the weekday (Mon, Nov 2 → Mon, Nov 9). The exact day is set in the dialog (Enter on a focused card). The Undo flash is illustrative: no data is saved.
