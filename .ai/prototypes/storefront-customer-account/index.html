Storefront Customer Account — screen inventory

Twenty-five portal screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-08-14-storefront-customer-account.md (rev 3) and its §15 story map. The shell mirrors packages/ui/src/portal/PortalShell.tsx; list and form content reuses the real backend .dt/table/form-layout classes, since portal pages are built from the same DataTable and CrudForm components as the backoffice. Colors and spacing come from globals.css. Every screen lists the spec sections and user stories it covers.

All data is fictitious. Four personas recur: Alex Morgan (B2C, own scope), Dana Ruiz (Ironclad Supply Co., company_admin and purchase_approver), Ravi Okonkwo (same company, company_summary) and Mira Halvorsen (a different company entirely).
