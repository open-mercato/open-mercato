Meeting booking — screen inventory

End-to-end flow for .ai/specs/2026-10-29-calendar-public-meeting-booking.md: the CRM user turns on their booking page (1–2), an external visitor books on the public page (3–8), the user handles requests on the Meeting requests page and in the CRM Calendar (9–18), and the booker manages the meeting from the e-mail link (19–21). This revision applies the UX review of 17 Nov 2026: settings cut to five essentials plus a collapsed Advanced section, a one-click Accept, three tabs instead of five, no bulk actions or counter tiles, and the error, empty, expired and narrow-screen states drawn. Backoffice screens are shown as of Fri, 30 Oct 2026, 11:30; public screens as of Thu, 29 Oct. All people and organizations are fictional.

1First run (not drawn): the page is off and every value has a default (30 min, Mon to Fri 09:00 to 17:00 in the browser's timezone, approval on, 60 days ahead, 2 hours notice, no buffer, no daily limit, booker changes allowed). Turning the switch on and saving is enough to share the link. The link name defaults to the user's e-mail local part and can be changed from the link's Copy menu (not drawn).
2Removed from the first version: "Start times every" (starts follow the meeting length), "Saved in CRM as" (always a meeting), "Buffer before", "Respond within" (fixed at 24 h), the self-service cutoff (fixed at 1 h before the meeting), "Max new requests per day" (an admin/system abuse limit, not a per-user setting), date overrides (block a day off with a calendar event), multiple ranges per day and "Copy Monday to all weekdays".
3Advanced is collapsed by default and its summary line shows the current values, so the whole configuration reads without opening anything. The two booking-window values are edited in place inside the sentence. Save uses optimistic locking (conflict bar on 409).
4The info alert answers the external-calendar question. This phase is for people who manage their calendar wholly in Open Mercato, or who block their Google or Microsoft calendar time by hand. Integration with external calendar providers is out of scope until calendar connectors exist in the codebase; once a connector syncs events into the CRM, they block times without changes here.
5The page needs customers.booking.manage_own, not customers.interactions.manage. Not drawn: the warning alert shown here and on Meeting requests when booking maintenance is not scheduled or was disabled by an admin.
6On a phone (below 640px) the page is one column with no horizontal scroll: the link wraps onto its own line with full-width Copy and Open, each label sits above its summary and control, and the info alert and next times follow the form. Cancel and Save stay pinned to the bottom.
