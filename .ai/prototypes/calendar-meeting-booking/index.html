Meeting booking — screen inventory

End-to-end flow for .ai/specs/2026-10-29-calendar-public-meeting-booking.md: the CRM user configures their booking page in the backoffice (1), an external visitor books on the public page (2–5), the user handles requests and sees them in the CRM Calendar (6–9), the booker manages the meeting from the e-mail link (10–11), and the user reviews all meeting requests in one place (12). Backoffice screens mirror the backend shell, CrudForm and DataTable anatomy from packages/ui/src/backend/; public screens deliberately have no backend chrome but use the same design-system primitives and tokens. Backoffice screens (1, 6–9, 12) are shown as of Fri, 30 Oct 2026, 11:30; public screens as of Thu, 29 Oct. Trimmed to the main journey — validation, empty, 404, decline, expired-on-accept and past-cutoff states are described in screen notes. All people and organizations are fictional.
