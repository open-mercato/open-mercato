Availability Contract — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-08-14-availability-contract.md and its §17 user story map. Only Epics A (sell-policy configuration) and B (diagnosing states) have a defined backend UI (§12) — Epics C and D are internal service contracts with no admin screen anywhere in the spec, so they are not illustrated here. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. All records are synthetic (tenant "Northwind Fasteners Co."). Every screen lists its requirement and user-story coverage.
