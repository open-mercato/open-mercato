Property Management — screen inventory

Backoffice and customer-portal screens for the property_management core module: the floor plan with its unit panels, the floors with their spaces and units, and the Buildings, Leases and Bookings records behind it. Derived from .ai/prototypes/property-management.requirements.md. The shell, page scaffolding, and status/drawer components mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. The floor-plan grid itself is a new composition proposed by this prototype — see the note on screen 1. Every screen lists its requirement and user-story coverage.
