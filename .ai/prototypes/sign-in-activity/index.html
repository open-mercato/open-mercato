Sign-in activity — screen inventory

Clickable low-fidelity screens derived from .ai/specs/enterprise/2026-11-13-sign-in-activity.md (stories US-A1, US-A2, US-U1, US-U2). All people, emails and IP addresses are fictitious; IPs use the RFC 5737 documentation ranges. Filters, the drawer and the notification panel work in the page; nothing calls a server.
