Checkout funnel — screen inventory

Twenty-one screens derived from .ai/specs/2026-03-19-checkout-simple-checkout.md and its §20 story map. Two surfaces, drawn differently on purpose. Screens 1–18 are the buyer's funnel and use storefront chrome — a deliberate, explicitly requested exception to om-mockup-prototype's backend scope. Screens 19–21 are genuine backend surfaces and use the real AppShell.

Most of this prototype is refusals, because most of this spec is. Every precondition failure is cheap and leaves nothing to undo, and each screen names what happened and what the buyer can do instead. The four workflows processes of §5.1 all run past the point where the buyer is waiting; they are noted on screens 20 and 21, not drawn. The four risks with no state of their own — R1, R3, R7, R10 — are named in §20 rather than left to look missing.
