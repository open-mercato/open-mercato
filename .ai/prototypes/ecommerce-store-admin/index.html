Ecommerce Store Admin — screen inventory

Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/specs/SPEC-029-2026-02-17-ecommerce-storefront-module.md §10a (User Stories) and §11 (Admin UI). Scope is the admin/backoffice surface only — store directory, general settings, branding, domain bindings, channel bindings and SEO defaults. The shopper-facing storefront is a separate spec (10) and is not represented here. The shell, page scaffolding, DataTable chrome, tabs and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colors and spacing come from globals.css. Every screen lists its requirement and user-story coverage.
