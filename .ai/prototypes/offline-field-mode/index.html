Offline Field Mode — screen inventory

Interactive storefront screens (buyer-facing, apps/storefront/src/app/field/*) derived from .ai/specs/2026-09-22-offline-field-mode.md and Epic F (.ai/specs/2026-08-14-storefront-app.md §16). This is not the backend AppShell/DataTable chrome — /field/* is a separate, client-only, mobile-first route subtree with no admin sidebar or topbar (spec §3.4). Colors, spacing and components still come from the same design tokens (globals.css). Every screen lists its spec section and user-story coverage.

1. Enable field mode

Field mode is off by default (OfflinePackPolicy.enabled: false). A buyer must explicitly opt in and see what will be stored, and for how long, before anything downloads.

§6§8US-F1
/field

Field mode

Work offline in the warehouse or field

Download a priced slice of the catalog now, queue items while you're offline, and sync the moment you're back.

What gets stored on this device

Up to 500 products from your price list, encrypted at rest
Kept for 72 hours, then purged automatically
Locked behind Face ID / PIN — every time you open field mode

You can turn this off and clear stored data any time from Status.

Enable field mode?

This downloads a priced catalog for your account only and stores it, encrypted, on this device for up to the TTL below.

No offline checkout

Prices update the moment you're back online — nothing here is a locked-in price or a placed order.

2The TTL choice writes to OfflinePackPolicy.offlineTtlHours for this buyer's device only; the store-level default and hard floor are merchant-configured (§4.1).
