## Assumptions

- Seeding credentials for the test channel is the intended fix, rather than exempting `__test_seed__` from credential resolution. Chosen because it leaves production logic untouched and makes the seeded tenant represent a *properly configured* tenant, which is also the only one of the three credential cases the integration suite currently never exercises. The alternative is a one-line guard in `system-email.ts` and stays available if a reviewer prefers it.
- The credential shape `{ testSeed: true, fromAddress }` is correct, because it is exactly what the test-seed provider's own `resolveCredentials` returns in `ensureTestSeedAdapterRegistered()`.
- The `resend` channel row seen in the ephemeral database is inert for this path, because `findTenantSystemEmailChannel` filters by `providerKey` and `SYSTEM_EMAIL_PROVIDER` is `__test_seed__` there.

## Risks

- The same shape — a channel row without credentials — can plausibly occur on a real instance that upgraded onto the Communications Hub. If so, this fix makes CI green without addressing the production consequence, and the design question in Non-goals becomes the more important one. Flagged for the reviewer rather than silently resolved here.
- Only the three named tests are verified against; other suites touching invitation email may reveal further fallout under the full gate.
