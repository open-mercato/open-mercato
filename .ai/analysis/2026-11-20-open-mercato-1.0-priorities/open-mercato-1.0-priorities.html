Executive summary

  • 58 P0 blockers, 61 P1 and 27 P2 items in 7 focus areas. 21 business-module decoupling bugs move to the #9678 extraction track, 14 business issues are explicitly excluded, and 25 issues from the earlier analysis are already closed.
  • 21 of the 58 P0s already have an open fix PR or are in progress. The fastest route to 1.0 is review and merge throughput on those PRs. Most of them are security and CRUD-factory fixes.
  • The CRUD factory is the highest-leverage security target (#9016, #9896, #9015, #9154, #9106, #9139). Every module, including third-party ones, inherits these bugs, so one fix in packages/shared/src/lib/crud hardens the whole platform.
  • Kernel-only apps do not work yet. auth, configs, entities, workflows, data_sync, scheduler and record_locks all fail when a peer is disabled, and the ephemeral test env cannot even boot without customers (#9311). #8784 (dependency integrity, spec merged) is the plan to adopt for this.
  • Freeze the extension contract deliberately. Spot IDs (#4633), mutation-guard bypasses (#9710, #7674), override remounts (#5194) and generic CrudForm section overrides (#6686 via spec PR #6769) all have to settle before BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md freezes them.
  • Contract decisions to make before 1.0: payload casing (#1821), command-ID naming (#1784), whether api_docs stays public (#3808), undo scope (more than 40 module-level undo conflict bugs come from one generic gap, #8873), and the per-request DI cost (#8989).
  • Relabel the road-to-v1.0.0 issues. 5 of the 6 open ones are business-module issues (#8423, #8404, #8221, #8140, #8421). Under the kernel-only vision only #8258 belongs there.
