## What changed since round one

The round-one fixes mostly held, and what remains is narrower. Round one verified 6 critical and 62 high issues. Round two finds 3 new critical and 32 new high issues, and 6 round-one fixes that did not fully hold. Most of the large medium count is round one's 175 open medium issues, which were never addressed (163 of round two's issues repeat open round-one items).

The new problems fall into five groups.

### 1. Six round-one fixes did not fully hold

- **g4-01, a round-one critical.** The organization-scope check in the DSR engine is placed in a `makeCrudRoute` hook that is skipped on the command path the holds use, and policy delete is not covered (r7-02). The fix needs to name the right seam: a guard in each route handler, or the command's own check.
- **g4-04.** The DSR engine gives two contradictory definitions of a stale hold (r7-01).
- **g1-03.** The dev-runner mark can still be satisfied from the environment alone (r1kk-04).
- **g1-04.** The SSO and inbound-sender hash fill commands are relied on but never specified (r1kk-02).
- **g5-05.** The engine side of `--related include` still erases a related subject nobody reviewed (r8-04, r7-04).
- **g2-05.** A plan step of the erasure spec still describes the old lock order (r3e-25).

### 2. Some round-one revisions introduced new problems at their seams

These come directly from changes made after round one, including the coordinator's own fixes.
- **Form-to-deals (critical r0-01, and r6-07).** The subscriber now acts only on `reason: 'request'`, so the new `erasure` reason, emitted by the C4 erase run, is ignored, and the CV and mapped answers stay on the deal.
- **Chat file release by id (r6-02).** It can release a file that another conversation still lists, because the ids come from the client.
- **Pending file deletions (r6-05).** Nothing drains them for the conversations set when the enterprise engine runs it.
- **The contract's registry field (r4-02).** It is described as carrying only data classes. `doctorChecks`, `tableExports`, `retentionSets` and the other new members all rely on the whole default export reaching the registry.

### 3. Audit history is still the leakiest store

Two of the three new critical issues and several highs come back to action-log payloads and undo:
- **Participant clean-up (critical r3e-01).** It edits other people's interactions, but leaves the erased person in those interactions' audit entries, and an ordinary undo writes them back.
- **Forms, documents and workflows (critical r6-01).** Their erasure leaves the cleared data in action-log payloads.
- **Undo during the run (r3e-02).** Undo and redo stay usable until the history step runs.
- **Blanked retained orders (r5-06).** They keep the buyer block in later audit entries.

One rule closes most of these. Every class's erase returns, as history records, every record it changes, including other people's records it edits. The run then blocks undo and redo for those records from its first write, not its last step.

### 4. Settings and checks that cannot exist yet

Retention ages that coverage specs rely on have no way to be set: the sales, checkout and staff ages (r5-04, r5-05). Several doctor checks need database reads that the doctor spec forbids (r2iqa-02, r5-04). The spec set needs one place where tenant privacy settings live, and either a database-reading doctor tier or a different home for those checks.

### 5. Legal holds are still uneven across paths

- **OSS has no holds**, but the program's Known limits say a hold stops erasure and retention (r0-02). Forms retention ignores holds as well (r8-03).
- **A hold on an email address** does not protect a retained order linked to a person (r5-02).
- **The agent retention set** deletes processes about a held customer's deal (r6-03).
- **Replay** cannot complete an entry for a subject the restore removed (r7-03).

### Suggested next step

1. Fix the 3 critical issues and the 6 incomplete round-one fixes. They are small, local changes, except the history rule in group 3, which belongs in the erasure spec once.
2. Then fix the 32 new high issues by the same file-owning method.
3. The open medium issues from both rounds (round one's 175, and 125 new) are mostly contained gaps and missing tests. A single pass per spec, by the spec's own owner, is the cheapest way through them.

### r1kk-04 · HIGH · repeats round-one g1-03; round-one fix incomplete · The development-runner mark can still be satisfied from the environment alone

## Critical issues

### r0-01 · CRITICAL · Form-to-deals erasure subscriber ignores the `erasure` reason, so a C4 erase run leaves the CV and mapped answers on the CRM deal

- **Where:** [2026-06-17-form-to-deals:53](.ai/specs/2026-06-17-form-to-deals.md:53) — raised by Opus, Sol, DS-Flash (confirmed)
- **Problem:** The revision makes the form-to-deals subscriber act only when `forms.submission.anonymized` carries `reason: 'request'` and acknowledge everything else with no writes. The content-ai coverage spec, in the same revision set, gives the forms anonymize service an `erasure` mode whose event reason is a new value, `erasure`.
